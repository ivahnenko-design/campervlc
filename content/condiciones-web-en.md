1.1. The sole purpose of the contract is the rental of the motorhome described, together with the equipment forming part of it. 1.2. The contract is governed by Spanish law. No clause of this contract limits the rights that consumer and user legislation grants to the Lessee on a non-waivable basis.

1.3. The rental period is the one agreed in the Particular Conditions. Tacit extension through continued use is excluded. Any amendment to the contract shall be made in writing (including by e-mail, messaging with acknowledgement of receipt or through booking management on campervlc.com).

1.4. Only persons registered in writing before the vehicle is collected may travel in, sleep in and use the vehicle, up to the maximum number of authorised seats.

### 2. Minimum age and authorised drivers

2.1. The Lessee and each additional driver must be at least 25 years old and hold a current category B driving licence, held for more than 2 years and valid for driving in Spain. Non-EU residents must also hold an international driving permit together with their equivalent national licence.

2.2. If, at handover, the driver cannot show a valid licence, the Lessor may refuse to hand over the vehicle; the cancellation policy in section 4 shall then apply as a cancellation attributable to the Lessee.

2.3. Only the Lessee and the additional drivers registered in this contract may drive the vehicle.

### 3. Price, duration, handover and return

3.1. The price includes: VAT; mileage of 100 km per rental night (excess charged at 0.30 €/km), which may be increased through the mileage options purchased when booking (200 km per night or unlimited mileage); comprehensive insurance with a deductible of 900 €, or 600 € if the reduced deductible option is taken out (sec. 11); the chassis-cab manufacturer's mobility guarantee; two gas bottles; capsules for the chemical toilet; and free parking for the Lessee's private vehicle at the Lessor's premises during the rental (sec. 12.3).

3.2. At handover, the Handover Report (Check-In) describing the condition of the vehicle shall be signed and one set of keys shall be handed over. Loss or theft of the keys shall be invoiced at the actual cost of replacement and shipping, up to a maximum of 250 €. The Lessor may make handover conditional on completion of the briefing on how to use the vehicle.

3.3. On return, the Return Report (Check-Out) shall be signed. If the Lessee is not present at the inspection for reasons attributable to them (keys left in a drop box, failure to attend), the Lessor shall document the condition of the vehicle with photographs dated the same day and shall notify the Lessee of any damage found within a maximum of 48 hours, and the Lessee shall be entitled to submit representations.

3.4. Return time and late return. A return later than the collection time agreed in the booking is invoiced as a supplement: up to 6 hours over, 50 % of one rental day; more than 6 hours, one full rental day. This supplement is paid at the time of booking when the return time is chosen in the booking. Late return not agreed in advance shall be penalised at 50 € per hour, up to a maximum, for each day of delay, of the amount corresponding to 3 rental days; the Lessee must report any delay immediately and shall also be liable for the proven damage caused to the Lessor in relation to other customers. After 4 hours of delay without notice or justified cause, the Lessor may terminate the contract and report the failure to return the vehicle to the competent authorities, in addition to activating the GPS tracker (sec. 12.4).

3.5. Early return at the Lessee's own decision does not entitle the Lessee to a refund of the price, except as provided in sections 9 and 10 in the event of a breakdown not attributable to the Lessee.

3.6. Return at a location other than the collection location requires prior written agreement and payment of the amount agreed before collection.

3.7. Fuel: the vehicle is handed over with a full tank and must be returned the same way. Otherwise, 25 € shall be charged for each missing mark on the fuel gauge plus 10 € for the refuelling service. The Lessee bears the cost of fuel, AdBlue and oil during the rental.

3.8. Water and cleaning: the vehicle shall be returned clean inside, with the grey water tank empty, the toilet cassette (black water) emptied and clean, and the fresh water tank full. Otherwise: 20 € for the grey water, 90 € for the toilet cassette not emptied or not cleaned, and 20 € for the fresh water. Insufficient interior cleaning shall be charged at actual cost, up to a maximum of 150 €.

3.9. Filling the drinking water tank with fuel, or the fuel tank with water or any other unsuitable fluid, shall give rise to a charge for the actual cost of draining, cleaning and repair, evidenced by invoice, to be deducted from the security deposit and, insofar as it exceeds the deposit, payable by the Lessee.

3.10. Extension: must be requested at least 3 days before the end of the contract and is subject to availability and written confirmation from the Lessor.
